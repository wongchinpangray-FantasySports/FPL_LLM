Xavi Simons (knee), Wilson Odobert (knee) and Dejan Kulusevski (knee) are still out for Tottenham Hotspur, although fellow long-term absentee Mohammed Kudus (thigh) is “close”.

On Friday, Roberto De Zerbi gave mixed news on Pedro Porro, Destiny Udogie, Micky van de Ven and Dominic Solanke.

While Porro’s no-shows are down to a deep run in the World Cup, the other two defenders have been nursing minor issues. As for Solanke, he’s not featured since a 45-minute run-out against Chelsea on August 1. 

Two are fit, and two are not. But James Maddison and Matheus Fernandes, who featured in what was effectively a B-team friendly last weekend, will be involved.

“Micky [van de Ven] is not on the list tomorrow, like Pedro Porro. The other players are all inside [the squad list]. James Maddison is inside, Dom Solanke is inside, Udogie is inside, Matheus Fernandes is inside.” – Roberto De Zerbi

Pape Matar Sarr (abductor) is another addition to the injury list.

“Pape Sarr, no, because he felt pain two days in his abductor, I think. Nothing important but we don’t take risks with him.” – Roberto De Zerbi
