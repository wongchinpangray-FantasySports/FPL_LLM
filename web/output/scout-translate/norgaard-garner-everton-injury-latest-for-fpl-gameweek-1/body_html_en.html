Speaking at Friday’s press conference, David Moyes confirmed that Everton will be without three central midfielders.

James Garner underwent successful surgery to address a groin issue in July, while new boy Norgaard has gone under the knife for the same reason.

Tim Iroegbunam, heavily linked with a move to Hull City, is out with a groin problem of his own.
