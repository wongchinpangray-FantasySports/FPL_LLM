The first Fantasy Premier League (FPL) deadline of 2026/27 is nearing – so it’s time to nail down our Scout Picks for Gameweek 1.

While we still have some press conferences to come (and some unexpected news could alter these selections), the managers of eight of our XI have already faced the media on Thursday.

This is a Free Hit in all but name, with the focus solely on the upcoming Gameweek. Selections made in the Scout Squad are a big factor when it comes to deciding who made the final cut.

As ever, we are limited by certain restrictions:

  • An £83.0m budget for our starting XI
  • An overall squad limit of £100.0m
  • No more than three players per team

GAMEWEEK 1 SCOUT PICKS

GOALKEEPER

Bart Verbruggen (£4.5m) is our chosen goalkeeper after he was selected by two of our Scout Squad pundits.

Brighton and Hove Albion meet an Aston Villa side deprived of some key players (especially attackers), thanks to injury and plenty of exits in a summer of upheaval for the club.

Albion played in Europe on Thursday night, which is far from ideal.

However, they were the third-best team in 2025/26 for goals conceded (46), and the fifth-best for expected goals conceded (xGC, 49.13).

It’s also worth mentioning that Brighton kept clean sheets in their two most recent pre-season friendlies against Roma and Bologna, as well as Thursday’s draw with Tromso.

DEFENDERS

Gabriel Magalhaes (£8.0m) was in the running for Scout Picks duty this week. However, our £83m budget for our starting XI only stretches so far, so Cristhian Mosquera (£5.5m) works his way into our weekly selection instead.

The likely arrival of Ezri Konsa (£4.5m) from Aston Villa will soon add to Mosquera’s rotation risk, but as a short-term punt, he’s devoid of much competition against Coventry City.

Arsenal are the only team who sit over 50% in our clean sheet chances list this week:

Riccardo Calafiori (£5.5m) completes our Arsenal defensive double-up.

Unleashed as an attacking force in the Community Shield, with Coventry expected to sit deep, there’ll surely be plenty of opportunities for the Italian to roam into attacking areas and create the necessary overloads.

Harry Maguire (£5.0m) offers points potential at both ends of the pitch away to Hull City. The aerially dominant centre-back will be a natural target for set-pieces, particularly with Casemiro no longer around.

Also, our clean sheet chances list makes Manchester United second favourites for a shut-out in Gameweek 1, behind Arsenal.

MIDFIELDERS

Bruno Fernandes (£12.0m) and Bryan Mbeumo (£8.0m) were both backed by all four of our Scout Squad pundits.

Fernandes was pretty much the first name on the teamsheet this week. Seven double-digit hauls from Michael Carrick’s appointment onwards in 2025/26 were at least three more than anyone else. He was also the leading player for expected goal involvement (xGI) in that period (11.02).

With Hull 23rd for xGC in the Championship last season, we’ve also got a potentially ‘out of position’ Mbeumo in the Scout Picks. He’s returned in three of his final four friendly matches this summer, indicating that he can do well against Sergej Jakirovic’s side.

Rate My Team has Fernandes and Mbeumo as the two top projected midfield scorers of Gameweek 1:

Dominik Szoboszlai (£7.0m) has to face a Newcastle United side who will be without last season’s first-choice three central midfielders. They’ve looked suspect at the back in pre-season, too.

Helped by a role on set plays, the Hungarian was Liverpool’s leading chance creator in 2025/26, racking up 78 key passes. A tireless worker in the middle of the park, he’s also in the mix to take penalties.

Finally, another Arsenal option. There was always going to be a triple-up against Coventry, and we’ve ultimately sided with new arrival Christos Tzolis (£6.5m).

He’s looked really lively in pre-season, with two assists in last weekend’s Community Shield, which potentially bodes well for the Greek winger.

FORWARDS

Erling Haaland (£15.5m) was unsurprisingly seen as the best forward for Gameweek 1 by our Scout Squad panel. All four of our pundits selected him in the top spot in his position.

The leader in this week’s Captain Poll, Haaland scored 15 goals and supplied four assists in 17 home matches last season, averaging 7.4 points per start. With a further week of training under his belt, the Norwegian gets the nod for Sunday’s home encounter with Bournemouth.

After a superb pre-season under Xabi Alonso, Joao Pedro (£7.5m) is primed for Scout Picks duty in Gameweek 1. The Brazilian boasted a points-per-start average of 5.3 last season, and could better every other forward apart from Haaland for attacking returns, with 24.

Pedro could be facing Fulham at an opportune time, as the Cottagers may still be getting used to Alvaro Arbeloa’s methods.

Sunderland’s Brian Brobbey (£6.0m) rounds off our selection at newly-promoted Ipswich Town. With Dominic Calvert-Lewin (£6.0m) facing a potentially tricky trip to Oliver Glasner’s Nottingham Forest, he’s arguably the pick of the £6.0m forwards this week.

SUBSTITUTES

With just £17.0m left of our budget, we’re raiding the bottom of the barrel for bench fodder – but playable bench fodder.

  • Antonin Kinsky (£4.5m): A potentially tricky trip to Brentford awaits, but due to our limited £100m budget, the Tottenham Hotspur number one takes his place on the Scout Picks bench.  
  • Luke O’Nien (£4.0m): Looks set to start the season in Sunderland’s back four, as it looks likely that Nordi Mukiele (£5.5m) and Omar Alderete (£5.0m) will be on the bench at best after their respective fitness issues.
  • Issa Diop (£4.0m): The Ipswich Town centre-back could have plenty of defensive work to get through this week, which boosts his defensive contribution (DefCon) potential at home to Sunderland.
  • Regan Slater (£4.5m): Looks to be one of the best (and one of the few starting) £4.5m midfielders for Gameweek 1, having scored two goals and supplied two assists last season. Slater could also be in the mix for DefCons.
