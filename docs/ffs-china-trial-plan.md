# FFS × Faleague — China trial plan (internal)

**Term:** 1 Aug 2026 → 1 Jan 2027 (extend only if both agree)  
**North star:** Be a great Scout channel first; test light China Pro second.  
**Rule:** Never pitch Faleague Pro as a Scout Members substitute. Scout-localised content stays free + attributed.

---

## Energy split (Phase 1)

| Share | Focus |
|------:|-------|
| ~70% | Scout content, WeChat/XHS, attribution, CTAs, metrics |
| ~20% | Free Faleague habit (Squad Builder, digests, prep insights) |
| ~10% | Soft Insights Pro (narrow; learn, don’t shout) |

---

## Phase map

### Phase 1 — Trust & distribution (now → end of trial)
- Ship Scout-localised articles (Chinese, free, credit + link + Premium line).
- Push **FFS Premium** + **Team Rater** with tracked links.
- Collect & share monthly summary with Chris.
- Soft Insights Pro only (existing Pro insight pages); high-level user count to FFS.

**FFS success:** clicks, Premium/free signups, Team Rater, China views/awareness.  
**Your success:** reach + habit + early Pro learning.  
**Partnership success:** Chris sees a channel, not a clone.

### Phase 2 — Prove China “light / quick / value” (only if Phase 1 healthy)
- Keep Pro narrow; ladder = Free → Faleague Pro → FFS Premium.
- Share learnings with Chris; no EN Members clone; no competitor deals.

### Phase 3 — Evolve together (post-trial, if mutual value)
- Co-brand, formal channel, China pack, or complementary tier — negotiate from proof.

## Red lines (do not cross in Phase 1)

1. Do not put Scout content behind Insights Pro.
2. Do not market Pro as cheaper/better Scout Premium.
3. Do not partner with FFS direct competitors.
4. Do not skip the monthly summary once the trial is live.
5. Do not over-invest in Pro features before Scout metrics look healthy.

---

## Go / no-go toward Phase 2 (internal)

Lean **yes** only if most are true:
- [ ] Scout post views trending up or stable with real reach
- [ ] Meaningful outbound clicks to Premium and/or Team Rater
- [ ] Chris still constructive on the monthly summaries
- [ ] Pro (if live) not cannibalising Scout CTAs in messaging
- [ ] You still have capacity without starving Scout deliverables

If not: stay in Phase 1 — Free Faleague + Scout channel is already a valid model.
