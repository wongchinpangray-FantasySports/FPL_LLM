## Slide 2 — The market (realistic framing)

**Global FPL**
- Official FPL is the world’s largest free-to-play single-league fantasy product — **~11.5M managers in 2024/25** (widely reported season high; treat as order-of-magnitude, not a Scout KPI).
- Audience is global and still growing; English content saturates the serious segment.

**China / APAC context (careful language)**
- Broader **China fantasy sports** is large and growing (industry reports cite ~USD 1.2B revenue in 2024, high teens CAGR) — this is **not** “FPL China revenue.”
- Premier League fandom in Greater China is real; **FPL-specific China headcount is under-published**. We will not invent a number.
- What we *do* know operationally: Chinese managers need **language, timezone, and WeChat-native habits** that UK Twitter/Discord tools do not serve.

**Honest gap:**  
China FPL demand exists; **trusted Chinese-language research + tools + distribution** is thin compared to the English ecosystem Scout already leads.

## Slide 3 — The China gap (problem we solve)

| What Chinese FPLers need | What the English ecosystem offers | What’s missing locally |
|---|---|---|
| GW prep in Chinese | Excellent EN content (Scout, creators) | Localised, timely packaging |
| Tools (value, fixtures, set pieces) | Mature EN member tools | Bilingual product UX |
| Habit loop | Twitter / Discord / email | **WeChat · 小红书 (XHS) · short video** |
| Trust | Scout brand | A credible China partner who doesn’t dilute quality |

### Observed behaviour (important — say this clearly)

Chinese FPLers commonly **pull recommendations from overseas FPL creators and sites** (EN YouTube, podcasts, X, specialist sites), then act on them locally.

In parallel, **local Chinese creators / fans often translate or rewrite that overseas content** and repost it as feeds on **小红书 (Xiaohongshu / XHS)**, WeChat, Douyin, Bilibili, etc.

So China already consumes Scout-ecosystem *signal* — but often through:
- informal translation,
- delayed or partial packaging,
- **weak or missing brand attribution**.

**Implication for Scout:**  
The demand bridge already exists. The strategic question is whether Scout wants that bridge to stay **unofficial and uncontrolled**, or become an **attributed, quality-controlled China channel** (content + product) with a partner who already ships distribution.

Entering China cold is still expensive; partnering is lower risk — and also a brand-protection move.

## Slide 6 — Marketing plan & focus (our GTM)

**North star:** Grow a Chinese-speaking FPL community that habitually uses tools + returns every GW — then monetise lightly (Insights Pro / sponsorship), without gambling framing.

### Focus 1 — WeChat-first distribution (primary)
- Daily Chinese digests (injury / transfers / FPL community)
- Rotating **Best of Position** hooks → deep link to exact-price Insights
- Pre-season / GW1 draft content for managers in Beijing time
- Mini program as the re-entry shell

### Focus 2 — Product-led retention
- Connect FPL Entry ID → personalised planner / captain / transfer flows
- Insights as the “research desk”; AI chat as the “analyst”
- Keep free core strong; Pro for market & model depth

### Focus 3 — Content flywheel (where Scout fits)
- Replace informal “translate-and-repost” with **authorised, attributed** Chinese packaging
- Meet managers where they already browse: WeChat + XHS (and related feeds), not only EN Twitter
- Always attribute sources; never strip Scout brand equity
- Measure: click-through to Insights, Entry ID binds, return visits (survey + product analytics)

### What we are *not* doing
- Competing for Scout’s UK membership narrative
- Claiming unverified “millions of China FPL users”
- Heavy paid ads before organic WeChat loops prove retention

## Slide 8 — Collaboration options (pick 1–2 to start)

Present as a **menu**, not a demand:

### A. Content & attribution partnership (lightest)
- Scout-approved excerpts / summaries localised into Chinese WeChat / XHS-ready cards
- Clear “Source: Fantasy Football Scout” + link back (vs today’s unofficial reposts)
- Pilot: 4–6 weeks of GW content · measure CTR; brand recall only in a **later** branded wave of research if needed

### B. Co-branded China Insights / research
- Scout editorial judgement + our bilingual Insights UI
- Example: “Scout Picks / Captaincy Matrix — China edition” hosted on Faleague ZH
- Scout retains brand control; we handle localisation + WeChat distribution

### C. Membership funnel experiment
- Soft CTA from Chinese content → Scout membership (EN) for advanced managers
- Or a China-priced / China-packaged offering if Scout wants to explore later
- Track with unique links + survey intent-to-subscribe

### D. Joint research (this survey)
- Run a bilingual FPL survey (China + EN) — **Scout brand hidden** to avoid bias
- Use results to **revise this deck into v2** (source mix, translation bridge, WTP in ¥, hub interest)
- Optional later: co-publish a short “State of FPL managers — China lens” with Scout

**Recommended opening move:** **D first (or A + D)** — survey → evidence deck → then scale content pilot. Low legal/product risk, fast learning.

## Slide 9 — What we’ve learned building this (founder experience)

Use as human credibility, not tech vanity:

1. **Optimisers first, LLM second** — rankings must be deterministic; AI explains, it shouldn’t invent.
2. **China is a distribution problem before a model problem** — WeChat cadence beats another EN feature.
3. **Trust travels with attribution** — Chinese managers already recognise quality EN sources; packaging + language is the unlock.
4. **Hub > single feature** — Insights + chat + Entry ID + daily card creates return visits.
5. **We’re early on measured growth** — product is live; rigorous market proof is what this collaboration + survey should produce.
